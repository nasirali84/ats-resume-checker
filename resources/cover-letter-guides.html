Cover Letter Writing Guide

How to craft targeted, compelling letters that capture recruiter attention in 30 seconds.

1. The Strategic Role of a Cover Letter

While your resume lists what you have done, your cover letter explains why you are the ideal fit for this specific company. It bridges the gap between your previous accomplishments and the employer's upcoming strategic goals.

2. Ideal Cover Letter Structure (The 4-Paragraph Rule)

  • Header & Greeting: Your contact info, date, hiring manager's name and title. Always avoid "To Whom It May Concern" if a manager's name can be discovered.
  • Paragraph 1 (The Hook): State the position you are applying for, how you discovered it, and a punchy 1-sentence value statement.
  • Paragraph 2 (Core Accomplishments): Highlight 1–2 quantifiable career achievements directly relevant to the role's primary responsibilities.
  • Paragraph 3 (The Company Connection): Explain why you want to work at this specific organization, referencing their culture, mission, or latest product milestone.
  • Paragraph 4 (Call to Action): Reiterate your excitement, express gratitude, and request a conversation.

3. Top Mistakes to Avoid

  • Never simply regurgitate your resume line by line.
  • Avoid sounding generic; a template sent to 50 companies with only the name swapped will be spotted immediately.
  • Keep the length under 350 words; hiring managers spend under 45 seconds scanning cover letters.
