Cookie Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026. How we use cookies and how you can control your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, enhance user navigation experience, remember user preferences, and provide analytical or advertising information to website operators.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

Our website uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential & Preference Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. For example, they remember your draft resume data in your browser's local storage so you do not lose your work when refreshing the page.
  • Analytics & Performance Cookies: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous traffic statistics, identifying popular career guides, and fixing technical bugs.
  • Advertising & Targeting Cookies (Google AdSense): These cookies are set through our site by advertising partners like Google. They are used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant advertisements across the internet. They do not store directly personal information like names or emails, but uniquely identify your browser and internet device.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our pages. Google uses cookies, such as the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other websites on the internet.

  • Google and third-party vendors use these cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of participating third-party advertising cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at networkadvertising.org or aboutads.info.

4. How You Can Control and Disable Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie rights by configuring your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies you have stored and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies or block all cookies from specific sites.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

To manage cookie settings in common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.

Please note: Disabling essential cookies may prevent certain interactive features of our Resume Builder from working as intended.

5. Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please re-visit this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed.
