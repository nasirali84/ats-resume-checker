Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about applicant tracking systems, resume scanning, and job application success.

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by employers, HR departments, and recruiters to collect, sort, scan, and rank resumes submitted for job openings. Common ATS platforms include Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, iCIMS, and BambooHR. Resumes that lack proper formatting or target keywords are often filtered out automatically.
An ATS score of 80% or higher is considered strong. It means your resume closely matches the qualifications, core competencies, and structure expected for the target position. A score between 65% and 79% is acceptable but has room for improvement, while scores under 65% indicate significant keyword gaps or formatting risks.
Both Word (.docx) and simple text-based PDF (.pdf) are widely supported by modern ATS systems. Word documents are the safest format for older legacy software. Text-based PDFs preserve your formatting across devices. Always avoid scanned PDFs or image-based files where text cannot be highlighted or selected.
