About ATS Resume Checker

Democratizing job search tools and leveling the playing field for candidates worldwide.

Our Mission

Over 98% of Fortune 500 corporations and a vast majority of modern employers utilize Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter job applications before a human recruiter ever sees them. Unfortunately, studies show that over 75% of qualified resumes are rejected solely due to formatting nuances, missing keyword variations, or unreadable document structures.

At ATS Resume Checker, our mission is simple: to empower candidates with the exact diagnostic tools and insights recruiters use, completely free of charge. We believe career opportunities should be determined by your skills, experience, and potential — not by your ability to outsmart automated parsing bots.

What Sets Our Tools Apart

  • 100% Free & Unrestricted: We never gate our scanner or builder behind deceptive subscriptions or credit card paywalls. You can check as many resumes as you need.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: Unlike legacy resume websites that collect, sell, or index candidate resumes into recruiter databases, our diagnostic engine runs directly inside your web browser. Your documents remain private and never leave your device.
  • Comprehensive 30+ Point Audit: We analyze structural headers, contact validity, action verbs, quantifiable metrics, word count sweet spots, and dynamic keyword match rates against target job descriptions.
  • Full Career Suite: Beyond our ATS scanner, we provide a modern Resume Builder, Cover Letter Generator, and deep educational guides on resume writing, interviewing, and career growth.

Our Technology

We combine client-side document processing standards (including in-browser PDF parsing and DOCX raw text extraction) with heuristic scoring models modeled on industry leaders such as Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Lever. By testing resumes against real-world ATS parser behaviors, we provide instant, actionable recommendations you can implement right away.

Our Core Values

Uncompromising Privacy

Your career documents belong to you. We respect your confidentiality and never store or monetize personal applicant data.

Universal Access

High-quality career advancement tools should be freely accessible to everyone, from fresh college graduates to executive job seekers.

Actionable Clarity

We don't just give you a generic score; we tell you precisely what words to change, what sections to add, and how to improve.
